## Problem the revision targets
People can't tell if a café will have a seat before walking over, and contributing an update felt like extra work. The revised flow puts live crowd + seating on the map card and makes sharing an update two taps per step, with a visible +10 points reward.

## Tasks (read aloud, don't demo)
1. "You want a quiet place to sit near you. Which café would you go to, and why?"
2. "Check whether Acro Coffee will be calmer later this afternoon."
3. "You're at Acro Coffee now. Let others know how busy it is."
4. "Use your points to get something at Acro Coffee."
